Postgraduate Opportunities

ZURLICA supports MSc and PhD research through thematic clusters, joint supervision, publication-oriented training, and collaboration with national and international partners.

Programmes

Research pathways

MSc Research Pathways

Thesis-based master’s research in Artificial Intelligence, Big Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Business Intelligence, and applied data science.

PhD and Doctoral Fellowships

Doctoral opportunities through internal fellowships, external scholarships, joint supervision, and sandwich research arrangements.

Research Clusters

Students work in thematic clusters rather than isolated projects, improving mentoring, publication output, and collaboration.

Joint Supervision

Co-supervision with partner universities, industry experts, government agencies, and regional research institutions.

Professional Certificates

Short courses in data analytics, machine learning, business intelligence, Python, dashboards, and responsible AI.

Career Pathways

Graduates are prepared for research, academia, data science, analytics, public policy, health informatics, fintech, and digital innovation roles.

Clusters

Research clusters for students

Prospective students may apply to work within one of ZURLICA’s thematic research clusters.

Health AI Cluster

Maternal health risk prediction, disease surveillance, clinical decision support, and health data quality.

Swahili NLP Cluster

Low-resource language models, speech technologies, misinformation detection, and public health communication.

Blue Economy AI Cluster

Fisheries analytics, coral reef monitoring, marine biodiversity classification, and coastal risk prediction.

Agriculture and Climate Analytics Cluster

Crop disease detection, yield prediction, climate resilience, and food security analytics.

Business Intelligence Cluster

Dashboards, organisational analytics, public sector performance measurement, and decision support.

Responsible AI Cluster

AI ethics, data governance, algorithmic fairness, policy analysis, and public trust.

How to apply

  1. Identify a ZURLICA research cluster aligned with your interests.
  2. Prepare a short research interest statement.
  3. Include your academic background and relevant skills.
  4. Contact the lab or a prospective supervisor.
  5. Complete formal university admission procedures if invited.

Typical requirements

  • Relevant bachelor’s or master’s degree.
  • Background in computing, mathematics, statistics, health, agriculture, marine science, business, or related fields.
  • Basic programming or data analysis experience is an advantage.
  • Strong motivation for research and publication.
  • Ability to work ethically with data and communities.

Funding

ZURLICA will support students through internal fellowships, external scholarships, industry-sponsored projects, government collaborations, and joint supervision with partner institutions where available.

Applicant Checklist

  • CV
  • Transcripts
  • Research interest statement
  • Preferred cluster
  • Writing sample, if available
  • Referee contacts
