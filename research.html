Research

ZURLICA advances scientifically excellent, socially relevant, and ethically responsible research in intelligent computing and analytics.

Priority Themes

Research areas

Our research portfolio is organised around themes where Zanzibar has unique needs, data opportunities, and regional relevance.

AI for Health and Public Health Analytics

Disease prediction, maternal health risk modelling, clinical decision support, health data quality, outbreak forecasting, and AI-enabled community health systems.

Blue Economy, Fisheries, and Marine Data Intelligence

Fish stock analytics, marine biodiversity monitoring, coral reef image analysis, coastal risk prediction, and sustainable fisheries decision support.

Agriculture, Climate Resilience, and Food Systems

Crop disease detection, yield prediction, climate-smart agriculture, spice value chain analytics, and food security early warning systems.

Swahili Language AI and Multilingual Analytics

NLP for Swahili and related languages, speech technologies, sentiment analysis, misinformation detection, and inclusive digital communication.

Tourism, Culture, and Creative Economy Analytics

Visitor behaviour prediction, sustainable tourism dashboards, heritage site monitoring, recommendation systems, and cultural data preservation.

Business Intelligence and Digital Governance

Public sector analytics, institutional performance measurement, data-driven policy, digital government services, and ethical AI governance.

Research pipeline

  1. Problem identification with local and national stakeholders.
  2. Ethics review and data governance planning.
  3. Data collection, cleaning, and documentation.
  4. Model development and evaluation.
  5. Reproducible analysis and peer review.
  6. Joint publication and open artefact release where appropriate.
  7. Policy, industry, or community translation.

Expected outputs

  • Peer-reviewed journal articles.
  • Conference papers.
  • Preprints and working papers.
  • Datasets and benchmarks.
  • Software tools and repositories.
  • Policy briefs and technical reports.
  • MSc and PhD theses.
