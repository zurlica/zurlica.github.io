About ZURLICA

ZURLICA is the Zanzibar University Research Lab for Intelligent Computing and Analytics. It supports research, postgraduate training, collaboration, joint publications, and applied analytics in Artificial Intelligence, Big Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Business Intelligence.

Who we are

ZURLICA is being developed as a collaborative research environment within Zanzibar University. The lab connects computing, statistics, public health, agriculture, fisheries, tourism, business analytics, and digital governance around shared research themes.

The lab is designed to strengthen Zanzibar University’s research capacity, increase joint publications, create postgraduate opportunities, and support evidence-based innovation in Zanzibar and the wider East African region.

Vision

To become a leading African research lab for intelligent computing and analytics, known for locally relevant solutions and globally recognized publications.

Mission

  • Conduct excellent interdisciplinary research.
  • Train MSc and PhD researchers.
  • Foster national and international collaboration.
  • Produce joint publications, datasets, tools, and policy outputs.
  • Translate data intelligence into societal and economic impact.

Core values

  • Research excellence
  • Ethical and responsible AI
  • Local relevance
  • Open collaboration
  • Interdisciplinarity
  • Capacity building
  • Integrity and inclusiveness

Governance

ZURLICA is guided by a Lab Director, Research Coordinator, Principal Investigators, a Scientific Advisory Board, and an internal Steering Committee representing relevant university offices.

Infrastructure

The lab develops compute, data, software, and collaboration infrastructure suitable for modern AI and analytics research. This includes GPU prototyping resources, secure data storage, reproducible workflows, version control, experiment tracking, and partnership communication systems.
