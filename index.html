About ZURLICA

A modern research lab for intelligent computing and analytics

ZURLICA brings together Artificial Intelligence, Big Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Business Intelligence to support research, publication, innovation, and advanced student training at Zanzibar University.

01

Research Excellence

ZURLICA prioritises scientifically strong, socially relevant, and ethically responsible research with potential for joint publications and regional impact.

02

Postgraduate Training

The lab supports MSc and PhD research through thematic clusters, joint supervision, and publication-oriented training.

03

Collaboration

ZURLICA collaborates with universities, government agencies, hospitals, industry, NGOs, and international research partners.

Research Themes

Focused on Zanzibar, Tanzania, East Africa, and the Global South

ZURLICA’s research themes combine local relevance with global scientific significance.

AI for Health and Public Health Analytics

Disease prediction, maternal health risk modelling, clinical decision support, and health data quality.

Blue Economy, Fisheries, and Marine Data Intelligence

Fish stock analytics, coral reef monitoring, coastal risk prediction, and sustainable fisheries decision support.

Swahili Language AI and Multilingual Analytics

NLP for Swahili and related languages, speech technologies, sentiment analysis, and misinformation detection.

Business Intelligence and Digital Governance

Public sector analytics, institutional performance measurement, data-driven policy, and ethical AI governance.
