Collaboration

ZURLICA collaborates with universities, research institutes, government agencies, hospitals, businesses, NGOs, and individual researchers.

Why Collaborate

Research partnership opportunities

Joint Research

Develop shared projects with clear publication targets and societal impact.

Co-Supervision

Supervise MSc and PhD students together through formal or informal partnerships.

Shared Datasets

Build ethical, documented, and reusable datasets for regional research.

Grant Proposals

Prepare consortium proposals for bilateral, multilateral, and foundation funding.

Partner categories

  • National universities and research councils.
  • Regional African universities and labs.
  • International research institutions.
  • Ministries and government agencies.
  • Hospitals and public health institutions.
  • Fisheries, agriculture, tourism, and environmental bodies.
  • Telecom, fintech, health tech, and analytics companies.
  • NGOs, foundations, and development partners.

MOU expectations

ZURLICA prefers outcome-oriented partnerships. A memorandum of understanding should define concrete deliverables rather than general cooperation only.

  • At least one joint grant application within 12 months.
  • At least one joint publication within 18 months.
  • At least one student co-supervision within 12 months.
  • One joint seminar, workshop, or training activity per year.
  • Clear data sharing, ethics, authorship, and IP terms.

Collaboration Types

  • Visiting researchers
  • Summer schools
  • Industry analytics
  • Policy research
